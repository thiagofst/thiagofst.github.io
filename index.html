I am a PhD candidate in Astrophysics at Yale University, currently in my fourth year of studies, focusing on primordial stars. My research is related to near-field cosmology and the history and structure of the Milky Way, with a particular focus on Population III and supernovae signatures in the spectrum of carbon-enhanced metal-poor stars, their oscillation patterns, chemical abundances, and white dwarf remnants. I also investigate the evolution of binaries, exoplanets, and substellar companions, especially in the context of the Milky Way's stellar populations in the thick disc and halo. Before coming to Yale in 2023, I completed an MSc in Astronomy at the Universidade de São Paulo (2023; IAG/USP, Brazil) and a **BS in Physics** at the Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina (2019; UFSC, Brazil).

I am always happy to discuss my work; feel free to visit my publications page or get in touch via email!
