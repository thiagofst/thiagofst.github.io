I am a 4th-year PhD candidate in Astrophysics at Yale University, and my research is broadly related to near-field cosmology and the history and structure of the Milky Way, with a particular focus on Population III stars, their supernova signatures in the spectra of metal-poor stars, and the evolution and oscillation patterns of low-mass stars. I also investigate the evolution of stellar binaries, exoplanets, and brown dwarfs/sub-stellar companions, specifically in the context of the Galaxy's stellar populations of the thick disc and halo.

I received an MPhil degree from Yale University in 2026, completed an MSc in Astronomy at the Universidade de Sao Paulo (Brazil) in 2023, and a BSc in Physics at the Universidade Federal de Santa Catarina (Brazil) in 2021.

I am always happy to discuss my work; feel free to read more about my research on my publications page or contact me by email!
