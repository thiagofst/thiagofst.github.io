Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a square or use the arrow keys to move between nuclides; the red bands mark the selected proton number Z and neutron number N.

Each element is coloured by its main cosmic origin, and split when several sources matter. This is a qualitative map, not a table of percentages: for many heavy elements the shares are still uncertain. A ? marks an element whose origin is actively debated. Categories follow Johnson 2019 and the chemical-evolution models of Kobayashi et al. 2020; r-process sites follow Cowan et al. 2021. In reality the s-process also has a minor weak component in massive stars, and a few proton-rich isotopes (p-nuclei) of the heavy elements come from other processes, which this map ignores.
